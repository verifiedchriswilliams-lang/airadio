# Eleven Radio: Buy List

## How to buy (Mac)

1. Open the **Music** app. If you don't see **iTunes Store** in the sidebar, go to Music → Settings → General and turn on **iTunes Store**.
2. Sign in with your Apple ID: Account → Sign In.
3. Click each link below. It opens the song in the iTunes Store. Click the **price** next to the song to buy it.
   - Buy the song, not the whole album. The link already points at the right version.
   - The iTunes Store has no shopping cart, so it's one click per song. Plan on about 30 minutes for the list.
4. Purchases download to your Music library as .m4a files (AAC 256 kbps, no copy protection). Live365 accepts them as they are.
   - If a song doesn't download automatically, find it in the sidebar under **Library → Songs**, then right-click → Download.
5. To find the files, right-click a song → **Show in Finder**. They live under `~/Music/Music/Media/Apple Music/` (on older setups, `~/Music/Music/Media/Music/`), one folder per artist.
6. Keep your Apple purchase receipts (emailed, or under Account → Account Settings → Purchase History). They're your proof of legitimate purchase for Live365.

Versions are already picked. Explicit is fine. Where an explicit version exists, the link points at it.

**Watch out for these:**
- Dracula: the original Tame Impala version, not the JENNIE remix
- Babydoll: the original 2018 version
- No One Noticed: the original, not the Extended or Spanish version
- Kids: the *Oracular Spectacular* version, not the 2005 EP
- Daft Punk Is Playing at My House: the studio version, not London Sessions
